About the Initiative

ASR Water & Drainage (ASR-WD)

An independent digital civic-tech platform enabling residents, grassroots organizations, and administrators to identify, map, and monitor drinking water and drainage infrastructure across Alluri Sitharama Raju District.

🛡️

Independence & Integrity Policy

ASR Water & Drainage is an independent civic technology platform. It is not an official government department and does not claim official endorsement unless explicitly adopted through signed institutional memorandums.

We never invent government officers, telephone numbers, or official resolutions. Citizen-submitted complaints are strictly labeled as Citizen Reported until verified on-site by authorized personnel.

1. Purpose & Mission

Clean drinking water and functional drainage are fundamental to community health and dignity. In topographically diverse regions like the Eastern Ghats of Alluri Sitharama Raju District, scattered hamlets often face pipeline breakages, seasonal contamination, or choked village drains that take weeks to be noticed.

This platform gives every resident an equal, direct digital channel to document issues with photographs and GPS locations, producing a transparent audit trail with verifiable progress updates.

3. Sustainability & Ethical Monetization

Citizen reporting and complaint tracking will always remain completely free. Citizens are never charged to report public water or drainage concerns. The platform sustains operations through legitimate institutional services:

A. Organization Subscriptions

Institutions, NGOs, and community housing boards subscribe for dedicated administration dashboards, custom analytics, and team dispatch tools.

B. Private Campus Management

Schools, tribal welfare hostels, residential societies, and hospitals can deploy private instances of the portal for internal facility management.

C. Maintenance Marketplace

For private household plumbing or septic tank cleanouts, residents can optionally hire verified local tradespeople, earning small referral fees.

D. White-Label Civic Software

Other districts and municipalities can license the platform codebase under their own identity with multi-tenant cloud hosting.
