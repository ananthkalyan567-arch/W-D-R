Citizen Data Protection

Privacy & Anonymization Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Platform Version 1.0 (ASR-WD)

1. Information We Collect

To ensure problem reports can be inspected and fixed by maintenance teams, we collect:

  • Location Information: District, Mandal, Village/Locality, Street or Landmark, and optional GPS coordinates.
  • Problem Details: Issue category, textual description, date started, severity rating, and uploaded photographic proof.
  • Citizen Contact Details (Optional): Name, phone number, or email address if you voluntarily provide them to receive verification updates.

2. Why Information Is Collected

Data is used solely to route water leakage, pipeline breaks, contaminated supplies, and drainage blockage notifications to the relevant field maintenance desks and engineering wings.

3. Public Anonymization Standards

🔒 Zero Public Exposure: A citizen's phone number, email address, or exact home identification is never displayed on the public Problem Map, Community Issues list, or public tracking timeline. Map coordinates are rendered at general vicinity level.

4. No Sale of Personal Data

We maintain a strict civic-tech ethos: We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize personal citizen records to marketing agencies, ad brokers, or commercial data aggregators.

5. Who Can Access Your Full Details

Only authorized, authenticated system administrators and assigned field department liaisons can access phone numbers for the purpose of confirming site access or validating problem resolution.

6. Data Retention & Inquiries

If you wish to correct or delete an erroneously submitted complaint or personal record, you can contact the technical desk via our Contact Civic Desk specifying your Complaint ID.
